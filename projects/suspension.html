01 / Design / Integration

Suspension architecture & packaging

Connecting the inboard and outboard suspension into a complete Formula SAE system, from assembly design and packaging to component stress checks.

Team / Role
Husker Motorsports Formula SAE
Suspension / Vehicle Dynamics Director
Project focus
Assembly integration & component design
Tools & methods
SolidWorks · FEA · Suspension kinematics

01 / Context

Project overview

The suspension connects tire behavior to the rest of the vehicle. My work with Husker Motorsports brings the component designs together into an assembly while accounting for suspension kinematics, packaging clearances, and manufacturing.

The scope includes rockers, pushrods, control arms, uprights, hubs, and mounting components. I work across both the inboard actuation system and the outboard hardware so their interfaces can be evaluated together.

02 / My role

My contribution

  • Directed the design and integration of the inboard and outboard suspension components.
  • Designed a decoupled inboard suspension actuation system in SolidWorks.
  • Performed component-level finite element analysis (FEA) to check stress against required factors of safety.
  • Checked packaging clearances and maintained engineering documentation for design changes.

03 / Approach

Engineering process

  1. Connect the component designs

    Use the assembly model to bring rockers, pushrods, control arms, uprights, hubs, and mounting hardware into the same system. This is where component design and suspension integration meet.

  2. Check packaging and stress

    Evaluate inboard packaging clearances and perform component-level FEA. Clearance checks address how parts fit together; the stress checks address component loading against the required factor of safety.

  3. Carry the design into manufacturing

    Connect assembly and component work with drawings, machining, and revision control. Keep changes documented so the design history remains traceable.
