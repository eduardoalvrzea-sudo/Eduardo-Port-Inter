03 / Projects

A closer look at the work.

Three areas of my work with Husker Motorsports Formula SAE.

Design / Integration

Suspension architecture & packaging

Bringing inboard and outboard suspension components together into a complete, clearance-checked system.

  • SolidWorks
  • FEA
  • Suspension kinematics
Engineering details +
  • Directed the design and integration of rockers, pushrods, control arms, uprights, hubs, and mounting components.
  • Designed a decoupled inboard suspension actuation system in SolidWorks.
  • Performed component-level FEA to verify stress against required factors of safety and checked inboard packaging clearances.

Analysis / Vehicle dynamics

Tire behavior & setup analysis

Using Formula SAE Tire Test Consortium data to connect tire behavior with suspension and vehicle setup decisions.

  • MATLAB
  • Tire-data analysis
  • Handling balance
Engineering details +
  • Developed a MATLAB script to compare lateral force, slip angle, cornering stiffness, pressure, inclination angle, and vertical load.
  • Used the relationships in the tire data to inform tire selection and setup decisions.
  • Analyzed load transfer, suspension kinematics, and handling balance as part of vehicle dynamics development.

Manufacturing / Documentation

Machining & design traceability

Manufacturing components to print specifications, with engineering documentation that keeps design changes traceable.

  • CNC machining
  • Manual mill & lathe
  • Revision control
Engineering details +
  • Operated CNC mills and lathes, as well as manual mill and lathe equipment.
  • Manufactured components to drawing specifications and tight tolerance requirements.
  • Maintained engineering documentation and revision control to track design decisions and change history.
